Research interests

Algorithms, information & dynamics.

01

Quantum algorithms & advantage

Designing quantum algorithms and understanding when quantum computation can offer an advantage over classical methods.

02

Open quantum systems

How quantum systems relax, how typical behavior differs from the worst case, and how rare states can set the pace of mixing.

03

Quantum learning

What measurement data can reveal about noise and dissipation, and the fundamental limits of learning quantum dynamics.

04

Quantum error mitigation

Methods for extracting useful quantum information in the presence of noise, including density matrix vectorization.

Journal articles and work on arXiv

2026Journal article

Decoherence-free quantum error mitigation by density matrix vectorization

Zhong-Xia Shang, Zi-Han Chen, Cai-Sheng Cheng

Physical Review Research 8, 023206 (2026)

Journal ↗arXiv ↗PDF ↗
Research summary

A quantum error mitigation approach that encodes noisy density matrices as virtual pure states. The protocol combines measurements and classical postprocessing for use in variational quantum algorithms, without requiring a known noise model.

2026Preprint

Typical Mixing and Rare-State Bottlenecks in Open Quantum Systems

Caisheng Cheng, Ruicheng Bao

arXiv:2605.07619 [quant-ph]

arXiv ↗PDF ↗Code ↗
Research summary

Typical relaxation curves can concentrate sharply even when rare initial states determine the worst-case mixing time. This work connects that separation to overlaps with slow modes and identifies regimes with markedly different typical and worst-case behavior.

2026Preprint

Physically natural metric-measure Lindbladian ensembles and their learning hardness

Caisheng Cheng, Ruicheng Bao

arXiv:2601.01806 [quant-ph]

arXiv ↗PDF ↗
Research summary

Physically motivated ensembles of random Lindbladians provide a setting for studying the difficulty of learning open-system dynamics. Statistical-query lower bounds connect limits on learning with potential cryptographic applications.
